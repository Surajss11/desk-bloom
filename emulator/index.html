Desk Bloom

A hands-first focus ritual for your real desk. Pinch a task card into the ring, focus, and grow a plant that is still there tomorrow.

Checking WebXR support...

  1. Sit at your desk. Desk Bloom finds the table and sets up on it.
  2. Pinch a card and drop it into the ring around the plant.
  3. Focus. The ring fills, then your plant grows.
  4. Drop finished cards into the jar. Breathe between blocks.

No headset? Try the emulated version in desktop Chrome (Immersive Web Emulator built in).
